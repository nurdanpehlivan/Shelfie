**Full description:**

📸 FOTOĞRAFLA EKLE
Buzdolabının, rafının ya da market fişinin tek bir fotoğrafını çek; ürünler son kullanma tahminleriyle birlikte otomatik eklensin. Barkod okutabilir ya da elle de ekleyebilirsin. Ürünü dondurunca raf ömrü uzar, çözünce kısalır, paketi açınca tarih kendiliğinden güncellenir. Yanlışlıkla mı bastın? Tek dokunuşla geri al.

🗓️ HAFTALIK PLAN VE ALIŞVERİŞ LİSTESİ
Haftanın öğünlerini tariflerinle planla, eksik malzemeleri tek dokunuşla alışveriş listesine ekle. Liste reyonlara göre düzenlenir; aldıklarını doğrudan kilerine aktar.

👨‍👩‍👧 AİLEYLE PAYLAŞ

📊 İSRAFI NE KADAR AZALTTIĞINI GÖR
Zamanında tükettiklerini, çöpe gidenleri ve tahmini tasarrufunu haftalık grafiklerle takip et.

🌍 SENİN DİLİNDE
Türkçe, İngilizce, Almanca, İspanyolca ve Fransızca. Ürün adları ve tarifler de seçtiğin dile çevrilir.

🔒 GİZLİLİĞİN ÖNEMLİ
Hesap açmadan kullanabilirsin; verilerin telefonunda kalır. Reklam yok, veri satışı yok.

Daha az israf, daha çok lezzet. Frizzy mutfağında seni bekliyor! 🥬

## English (en-US)

**App name (30):** Frizzy: Smart Pantry & Recipes

**Short description (80):** Snap your fridge, track what's fresh and cook it before it goes bad.

**Full description:**

Frizzy is the smart pantry app that helps you eat what you buy instead of throwing it away. Our fridge mascot Frizzy keeps an eye on your fridge, freezer and pantry for you.

📸 ADD FOOD WITH ONE PHOTO
Snap your fridge, a shelf or a grocery receipt and items are added with estimated use-by dates. Scan a barcode or add by hand too. Scan the date printed on a pack to use the real one. Frizzy also spots food that looks spoiled or has expired and warns you.

⏳ FRESHNESS AT A GLANCE
A green, amber and red bar shows what's safe and what to eat soon. Get a daily reminder for food on its last day, and a home-screen widget with what runs out first.

❄️ FREEZE, THAW, "I OPENED IT"
Freezing extends the date, thawing shortens it, opening a pack updates it on its own. Tapped by mistake? Undo in one tap.

🍳 RESCUE RECIPES
Get quick, real 15-minute recipes from what's about to spoil. Cook step by step with built-in timers and find a video of the dish in one tap. Pick any products you have and get a recipe for exactly those; save leftovers back to your pantry.

🗓️ WEEKLY PLAN & SHOPPING LIST
Plan the week's meals with your recipes and add what's missing to your list in one tap. The list is sorted by aisle; move what you bought straight into your pantry.

👨‍👩‍👧 SHARE WITH YOUR FAMILY
With one invite link, your whole household shares the same pantry, shopping list, recipes and weekly plan. See it all on the web panel too. When someone adds, uses up or throws something away, everyone gets a notification right away and sees it in the household's recent activity.

📊 SEE HOW MUCH YOU SAVED
Track what you ate in time, what was thrown away and your estimated savings, week by week.

🌍 IN YOUR LANGUAGE
English, Turkish, German, Spanish and French. Item names and recipes follow the language you pick.

🔒 YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS
Use it without an account and your data stays on your phone. No ads, no data sales.

✨ FRIZZY PREMIUM
Free: 3 photo scans and 3 recipes a day, and family sharing for 2. Premium: 50 scans and recipes a day and family sharing for up to 6 – for everyone in your household.

Waste less, cook more. Frizzy is waiting in your kitchen! 🥬
